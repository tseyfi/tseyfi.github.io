Tolunay Seyfi

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Clemson University, where I work with the IS-WiN Laboratory.

My research interests include AI-native wireless systems, O-RAN, radio resource management, 5G/6G networks, non-terrestrial networks, and software-defined radio testbeds. I am interested in translating learning-based methods into practical and reproducible wireless systems.

Previously, I worked on cellular standards and wireless systems at Qualcomm. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Purdue University, advised by Prof. Aly El Gamal.
