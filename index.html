01 — About

I study how wireless networks can become open, intelligent, and adaptive.

I am a postdoctoral researcher in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Clemson University, working with the IS-WiN Laboratory. My research sits at the intersection of wireless communications, information theory, and applied machine learning.

I build and evaluate AI-native systems for O-RAN and next-generation networks. My current interests include learning-driven radio resource management, experimental 5G platforms, non-terrestrial networks, and the path from algorithms to reproducible over-the-air systems.

Before Clemson, I worked on cellular standards and wireless systems at Qualcomm. My broader experience includes NASA JPL, Lynk, and cofounding UniPhy Technologies. I received my Ph.D. from Purdue University, where I studied interference management in dynamic wireless networks.
